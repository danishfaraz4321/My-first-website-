Giza Pyramids History

The Giza Pyramids are among the most famous and oldest monuments in the world. They were built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt during the Old Kingdom period. The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for Pharaoh Khufu and is the only one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World that still exists today. These pyramids were not just tombs, they show the amazing engineering skills of ancient Egyptians.
